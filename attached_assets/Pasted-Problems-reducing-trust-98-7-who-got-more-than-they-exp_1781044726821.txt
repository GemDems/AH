Problems reducing trust
"98.7% who got more than they expected" — highly specific claim with no source.
"Trusted by thousands" — vague.
"#1 Marketplace" — difficult to substantiate.
"1035 live viewers" and "708 orders this hour" — if not real-time verified, visitors may see them as fake.
"$6.2M+ Saved" — needs proof or methodology.
Too many competing trust messages at once.
